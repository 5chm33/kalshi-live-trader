# Daily-temperature source-alignment study (pre-registered September 26, 2026)

**Purpose:** Test whether the public-data chain is actually usable for a small, fee-aware forecast strategy. This is a **read-only feasibility and calibration-data study**, not a live or paper order strategy; it uses no trading key and sends no order. The prior one-contract pilot lost $0.0276 net and is not to be restarted.

## Immutable first cohort and observation rule

- **Series:** `KXHIGHNY` only; **city/station:** derive the `CLIxxx` code and city from *every* current market rule, then verify the matching `cliId`, city, ICAO identifier, and IANA time zone in the [Weather Company portal's public daily report](https://weather.com/kalshi). Do **not** use a hardcoded city-to-coordinate mapping. Check each event's settlement-source metadata specifically; the series currently says *The Weather Company*. Its [daily portal](https://weather.com/kalshi) publishes official NWS/NOAA CLI figures relayed by TWC, marked Official/Preliminary/Revised; this does **not** authorize substituting a generic NWS grid forecast for the final report.
- **Local cutoff:** 18:00 `America/New_York` on the day **before** each target climate date. One sample at or after 18:00 and within **120 seconds** only; otherwise append an explicit missed/error record, never backfill the 18:00 quote later.
- **Study window:** September 26, 2026, before the first 18:00 EDT cutoff; last decision target October 3 local; retain the separate final-label collection tail through October 5, 2026, 22:05 UTC at latest. A study restart resumes its immutable first registration, date, and 18:00 cutoff. The first target date is September 27 local. At most one decision observation per target date; no post-cutoff opportunistic strike selection.
- **Universe:** all open, non-provisional markets in the one validated next-day `KXHIGHNY` event. Archive exact original `rules_primary`, `rules_secondary`, event and series fee/source metadata, market statuses and payout rules, timestamped independent Kalshi depth-one books, and reciprocal YES/NO asks and displayed sizes. The full first-book-start to last-book-receipt envelope must be **≤5 seconds**; otherwise append an error rather than describe the legs as one comparable market state. A one-sided book is a rejection, not a zero-cost trade. Snapshot receipt time is **not** a venue-created orderbook timestamp; no actual fills are inferred.
- **Forecast provenance:** extract the portal's `cliId` and ICAO station from the previous day's *already final* public report, look up that ICAO on `api.weather.gov/stations/{ICAO}` for its official coordinates, then follow `api.weather.gov/points/{lat},{lon}` to the NWS forecastHourly URL. Capture the raw forecast response, its `generatedAt` and `updateTime`, and all hourly periods belonging to the **next local calendar date**. Both timestamps must be at or before receipt and no more than **six hours old**. `max(hourly temperature)` is an **uncalibrated NWS grid proxy**, not a probability and not the station-specific final TWC/CLI maximum. If source mapping, 23/24/25 DST-aware hours, forecast freshness, or date is ambiguous, log an error and no forecast.
- **Labels:** only after the day, fetch the *same date* from `https://weather.com/kalshi/api/climate/primary?date=YYYY-MM-DD`; require the same station `cliId`, date, `isOfficial=true` and status `official` or `revised`. Separately fetch the recorded Kalshi event and require all archived markets to be settled with explicit payout fields before attributing a Kalshi resolution. If the public CLI report is preliminary, absent, later revised, or disagrees with venue payouts, preserve all versions as append-only evidence, mark the label unresolved, and revalidate it periodically through the fixed label tail. No outcome is inferred from a preliminary hourly METAR or from a forecast.

## Economic and statistical gates

The feasibility sample collects no hypothetical entries until a **station-specific, pre-cutoff, probabilistic model** is trained on *earlier resolved records* and frozen for a subsequent forward holdout. The raw NWS maximum is not itself a probability. Later, any proposed one-contract YES/NO rule must compare its calibrated payout probability to the **displayed executable ask plus current series-specific entry fee** and a frozen margin, use only displayed quantity ≥1, and report every considered/abstained market. It must count each **distinct climate date** as a trial, not every intraday tick or strike. Paper results remain *unfilled quote proxies*; measure realized P&L only from actual venue-confirmed fills and fees. At least 30 complete source-matched dates are needed before even freezing a calibration rule and at least another 30 subsequent dates before a preliminary out-of-sample comparison; this is **not** a statistical proof threshold or permission to auto-trade. Missing/ambiguous dates stay in the denominator. A positive first date or recent leaderboard is not evidence of an edge.

The decision cohort ends after seven local daily cutoffs regardless of whether 30 dates are observed, with at most two more days of final-label collection; therefore its expected conclusion is **data availability/semantics only**. No continuous real-money trading or repeat of the finished $2 pilot is authorized by this study. If any source becomes inaccessible or the settlement rules change, stop scoring and retain an explicit failure row.
