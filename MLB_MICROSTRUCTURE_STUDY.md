# Pregame MLB quote-feasibility study — registered before collection

**Status:** Public-data-only, no orders, no fills. This study is a prerequisite screen for a possible micro-market-making idea, **not** a profitable strategy, execution backtest, or permission to trade. It will run for at most seven days from launch, using the first 120 distinct future eligible `KXMLBGAME` events discovered after registration. A game is eligible for cohort selection only when the official event response lists exactly two mutually exclusive full-game winner markets, their original scheduled time agrees in both `rules_primary` strings, and at least 60 minutes remain before that scheduled time. All subsequent no-quote, outage, delay, postponement, and exceptional settlement observations stay in the cohort.

The audit records the exact series response with `fee_type`, `fee_multiplier`, settlement sources, and prohibitions; the event's fee overrides and market-specific rules; and depth-one YES/NO bid books for **both** team winner markets every 10 seconds from 60 minutes before the original start through one minute afterward. The `occurrence_datetime` field is **not** assumed to be first pitch: on one verified Sep 26 game it was three hours after the first-pitch time written in `rules_primary`. A strict parser accepts only explicit US time-zone abbreviations and otherwise excludes the event. Book observations from separate HTTP requests are timestamped individually; they are not atomic and do not establish fills or queue placement. Settled markets are checked later for actual Kalshi payouts and nonbinary exceptions.

**One fixed decision observation per game:** select the `book_snapshot` closest to 30 minutes before the original scheduled first pitch, within five seconds, validating both independently received books were within that window. Never substitute a later favorable quote when the decision sample is missing, one-sided, thin, invalid, paused, or subject to an API error. The bid-derived conditional paired mark is `1 - YES_bid_team_A - YES_bid_team_B - conservative_fee_A - conservative_fee_B`; require both winner markets' YES and NO sides, each intended YES bid with at least one displayed contract, current on-grid prices, and current exchange fee data. It is a hypothetical **two-fill** payoff after ordinary binary settlement, not an actual risk-free trade. For a non-direct account conservatively align the **combined contract price plus six-decimal-place model fee** up to the next cent on each hypothetical maker buy, and assume no future rebates. This also captures a sub-cent price-grid rounding fee even if the model maker fee itself is zero. The event/series fee data, not a global multiplier, controls estimates; e.g. the live `KXMLBGAME` series read on Sep 26 reported `quadratic_with_maker_fees` and multiplier `0.5`. Exchange `fee_cost` on real fills would supersede any estimate.

**Predeclared falsification screen:** after 120 distinct game events, require the **same** at least 80/120 decision observations to have two-sided >=1-contract depth persisting across the surrounding one-minute window **and** a conservative conditional paired mark of >=$0.01. The report requires the registered study end and a unique Kalshi settlement record for **every one of the originally selected 120 games**, without outsider substitution or nonbinary payout. Count outages, missing snapshots, status changes, postponements, nonbinary payout cases, and book-side gaps in their original denominator. If fewer than 120 games are listed or settled before the seven-day stop, label the study incomplete rather than lowering the denominator or continuing indefinitely. A pass would establish displayed **quote feasibility only**; it would say nothing about actual maker fills, queue priority, one-leg adverse selection, realized net P&L, or profitability. There is **no automatic trading activation** at any threshold.
